AI Assisted Cancer Mutation Visualization Platform

About the Platform

ONCOLYZER converts genomic mutation reports into interactive visual networks. It analyzes gene mutation patterns and generates a mutation galaxy showing relationships between detected cancer genes.

The platform estimates potential cancer stage indicators and provides clinical guidance suggestions including treatment direction, preventive care, and lifestyle recommendations.

This project demonstrates how bioinformatics visualization and AI-driven interpretation can transform raw genetic data into understandable insights.

Upload Mutation Report

Upload a genomic mutation report (.txt file) containing gene mutation data. The ONCOLYZE analysis engine will detect cancer-associated genes such as TP53, BRCA1, EGFR, KRAS and visualize their mutation network.

Once uploaded, the system performs gene recognition, mutation clustering, and visual galaxy generation. The final report will summarize mutation impact and provide treatment guidance.
